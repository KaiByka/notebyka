Making space for better work

2026.09.12 · 4 min read

Good work rarely arrives because we managed to fit one more thing into the day. More often, it shows up after something has been removed: a notification, a meeting, or the pressure to make every quiet moment productive.

I have started treating empty space as part of the work. A clear desk helps, but the more important kind of space is the gap between one thought and the next. That is where a problem can change shape.

Fewer open loops

At the beginning of a session, I write down the one thing I want to understand or finish. Not a list of ten tasks, just one direction. The constraint is useful because it makes distractions visible without needing much willpower.

The result is not always faster. It is usually calmer, which turns out to be more valuable. Work becomes something I can return to instead of something I have to chase.
