Learning in public

2026.07.19 · 3 min read

There is a useful difference between performing expertise and documenting curiosity. The first tries to look finished. The second leaves a trail that makes it easier to keep going.

I keep these notes short on purpose. A question, a small discovery, or an explanation in my own words is enough. The archive becomes a map of what I have been paying attention to.

The value of the rough draft

Writing before I feel ready gives an idea somewhere to live. A month later, I can return to it with better questions. Sometimes the note is wrong; that is useful too, because the correction has a starting point.

Learning in public is less about being watched and more about making progress visible to yourself.
